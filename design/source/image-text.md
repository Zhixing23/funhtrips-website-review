### C2. `subjects__塑膠循環經濟-6.png` — 塑膠循環經濟 Plastic Circular Economy

流程：Waste Management → Plastic Recycling → Shredding and Granulation → Research and Development → Product Development →（循環）"Resource Circularity and Recycling"

- **"Ocean Waste Certification Mark"**：Utilizes 80% recycled plastic derived from ocean and land waste to produce eco-friendly recycled composite wood. The manufacturing and recycling process is energy-efficient with a low carbon footprint, allowing for repeated cycles of recycling and reuse.
- **For every 100 kg of recycled composite wood, it can reduce 274 kg of carbon emissions.** Circular plastic wood reduces the extraction of Earth's forest resources, enhances forest regeneration, and reduces climate change factors.
- **Truly transforming recycled plastic into usable resources.** Plastic is a petrochemical product that does not naturally exist in the environment. Without proper treatment, even recycled plastic may end up being incinerated which generates air pollution. Our partner possesses patented technology to transform recycled plastic into reusable materials, and all the waste generated during manufacturing can be fully recycled and remanufactured.
