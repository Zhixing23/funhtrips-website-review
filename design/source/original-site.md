### 歡迎 Welcome！

知行學旅有限公司是設立於台灣，致力於推動聯合國永續發展目標的遊學（研學）公司，主要運營教育旅行與商務交流。我們擅長跨域合作，通過與旅行社、學校、企業組織建立夥伴關係，替客戶規劃和執行符合其課程或是交流目的的行程。

Fun Horizon Educational Trips is a Taiwan-based educational travel company dedicated to promoting United Nations Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs). We are specializing in designing and operating specialty educational trips and business exchange for schools and enterprises globally. Through strategic partnerships with travel agencies, corporations, and organizations spanning various industries, our itineraries and programs are thoughtfully customized to meet requirements of our clients.

### 我們的服務 Our Services

**IB CAS Trips**
創造力、活動、服務是國際文憑課程的核心之一， 我們所設計的CAS 行程不僅會符合IB 要求，同時也會融入至少兩個聯合國永續發展目標。
The CAS (Creativity, Activity, Service) programs we design will not only meet the IB requirements, but also cover at least 2 UN SDGs in every trip.

**服務學習 Service Learning**
如果您的學校或是機構不是IB 學校，但希望在學習旅行過程中融入服務元素，我們會分析您的需求，挑選合適的台灣在地組織共同規劃合作。
If your school or organization is not IB school, but you wish to implement service element into the trips, we will help to analyze your needs and select local partner organization to operate the trips.

**語言與文化 Language &Culture Trips**
我們的文化行程涵蓋台灣本地文化及華人歷史與文化。除了文化行程，我們亦提供亦提供短期華語語言與文化營隊，透過與台灣華語教學單位及專業教師的合作，設計符合您學生或是學校需求的語言與文化結合的營隊。
You may explore local Taiwanese culture as well as Chinese culture in our cultural programs. Besides cultural trip, we also provide short term Chinese language learning camps by collaborating with Chinese learning centers or professional Chinese teachers in Taiwan.

**科目考察 Subject Trips**
我們能夠替您設計各種符合您學校課程以及學生特定學習目標的學術旅行計畫，例如學科標準、課程目標，甚至跨學科項目等。
We can create a various academic travel programs that meet your school curriculums as well as your students' specific learning objectives, such us subject criteria, curriculum goals, or even interdisciplinary projects, etc.

**學術參訪 Academic Visit- For Higher Education or Business Exchange**
如果您的學術單位（高等教育）或公司計畫至台灣進行商務或是學術參訪，我們能夠替您設計符合您專業的主題性行程，尋找適合的參訪與的單位及組織，協助您進行在地的聯絡與接待。
If your academic institution (higher education) or company plans to visit Taiwan for business or academic purposes, we can design thematic itineraries tailored to your profession, identify suitable visiting units and organizations, and assist you in local contacts and reception arrangements.

**研習、演講、工作坊 PD, Speech, Workshops**
我們的人才庫裡有許多不同領域的專業講師，每位講師皆在該領域擁有合格證照，歡迎台灣企業或是台灣各地學校接洽。
We have recruited a diverse range of professional instructors from various fields in our talent pool. Each of them possesses qualified certifications in their respective areas. We welcome inquiries from local companies and schools in Taiwan.

## 2. 關於我們 `/about-us/`

### 我們是誰 WHO ARE WE?
知行學旅有限公司由擁有超過十年海外學校和非營利組織工作經驗的教師創立。他們在台灣和香港的國際學校及非營利組織中累積了豐富的教學和組織教育旅行的資歷。成立這家公司旨在為全球的學校與企業提供專業的教育旅行和商務參訪內容。

Fun Horizon Educational Trips is founded by teachers and NPO workers who are proficient in organizing educational trips for international schools and NPOs in Taiwan and Hong Kong for over 10 years. We established this company to provide specialty travel and business exchange programs for schools and corporations worldwide.

### 願景與使命 VISION AND MISSION
我們的願景是連結教育與企業界，透過客制的主題化旅行協助青年學子拓展視野、實踐所學、接觸全球議題、培養全球公民意識。
我們的使命是協助學校和企業設計符合其需求的遊學、學術交流或是商務交流行程，並協同旅行社執行，減少過程中的溝通時間成本，更好地達到旅行或參訪目的。

We aspire to connect education and business to help young students expand their horizons and practice what they have learned through travel. On the other hand, we hope to promote international cooperation and exchange between companies.
Our mission is to assist schools and businesses in designing itineraries or programs that align with their curricula or activity goals, and to collaborate with travel agencies to execute the plans. Such collaboration would reduce the time and cost of communication during the process, enhancing the effectiveness of clients' learning or business trips.

Cultural experience is the core of travel, and as such, all of our programs include various cultural activities. Besides traditional Chinese culture, we also feature local Taiwanese culture and indigenous cultures. We will tailor the itineraries and design activities based on the requirements of schools and students.

**學術參訪 Academic Visit - For Higher Education and Business Exchange**
串連學術單位、在地非營利組織以及企業，我們設計的學術參訪行程不只停留在拜訪層面，還會有進一步的交流及探索。透過產學合作以及有組織性的規劃，讓您的學術參訪行程能夠觸及多重面向，深入了解當地的學術、產業和社會文化情境，拓展您的學術視野並建立有意義的合作關係。

Connecting academic institutions, local non-profit organizations, and businesses, our designed academic visit programs go beyond mere visits and involve further exchanges and explorations. Through industry-academia collaborations and organized planning, your academic visit will touch upon multiple aspects, providing deeper insights into the local academic, industrial, and socio-cultural contexts. This will broaden your academic horizons and establish meaningful collaborative relationships.
